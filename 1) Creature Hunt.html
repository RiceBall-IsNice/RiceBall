🎮 PC CONTROLS

Movement: Use W, A, S, D keys to move (stops when released).

Aiming: Aim directed by your Mouse Cursor.

Skills: Left Click = [L] Move | Right Click = [R] Move

Switch Moveset: Press keys 1-0, -, =, [, ], ; or Q to cycle.


📱 MOBILE CONTROLS

Movement: Use the Virtual Joystick on the bottom-left.

Aiming: Aim is locked 75px above creature's head.

Skills: Tap on-screen [L] or [R] action buttons.

Switch Moveset: Tap any moveset directly in the right side list.
